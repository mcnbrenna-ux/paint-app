02

Three tiers of glass

Depth stops reading past three levels. Tier 1 for inline controls, tier 2 for panels, tier 3 for anything that stops the world. Each adds fill, blur, border weight and shadow together — changing only opacity is what makes glass look like a filter instead of a material.

Tier 1
Subtle

4.5% fill · 14px blur · chips, rows, inline controls

Tier 2
Default

7.5% fill · 26px blur · panels, cards, toolbars

Tier 3
Modal

11% fill · 44px blur · dialogs, popovers, sheets

Modifier
Iridescent edge
